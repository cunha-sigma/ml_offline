**About the client**

<u>the client</u> is the client's parent company’s self-service advertising platform built to make premium TV advertising more accessible, measurable, and performance-driven for advertisers of all sizes.

**What We’re Building**

Over the next 12 months, our top priority is standing up a real-time bidding and optimization system to power scalable performance advertising capabilities across the client.

Key initiatives include:

- A custom bidding agent integrated with the ad server infrastructure for real-time bid decisioning

- Improved expected value modeling to drive better optimization outcomes (e.g. purchases, installs, ROAS optimization)

- Proprietary pacing and bid-shading logic to improve efficiency and move toward true automation

- Forecasting systems for impressions, reach, and outcomes estimation to support planning and advertiser trust

- Scalable backend infrastructure to support real-time optimization and delivery mechanics

These are foundational capabilities for any competitive performance advertising platform and are critical to delivering measurable advertiser outcomes.
